Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: August 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to All Creator Tools. We are dedicated to respecting and protecting the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you access and use our suite of free online web tools.

2. Zero User Data Storage

Our platform operates on a privacy-by-design principle:

  • We do not require user account registration, sign-ups, or personal identity details.
  • All image conversions, PDF processing, text manipulation, and QR code generation happen directly inside your web browser via client-side JavaScript APIs (Canvas, PDF-lib, WebCrypto) whenever technically possible.
  • We do not retain, upload, or sell your processed documents or multimedia files.

3. Cookies & Advertising (Google AdSense)

We may partner with third-party vendors, such as Google AdSense, to display ads when you visit our website:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Analytics & Telemetry

We may use standard web telemetry tools (such as Google Analytics or Microsoft Clarity) to analyze anonymous website traffic patterns, screen resolutions, and browser compatibility to optimize page load speeds.

5. Third-Party Embeds & Services

Certain tools (such as Multi YouTube Live, Twitch Browser, or Kick Browser) embed content directly from third-party services using standard official iframes. Interacting with these external platforms is subject to their respective Terms of Service and Privacy Policies.

6. Contacting Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your experience on our website, you can reach out via our Contact Page or by emailing support@pooreyoutuber.com.
